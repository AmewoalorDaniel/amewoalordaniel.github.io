I am a biomedical scientist interested in integrating experimental (wet lab) and computational (dry lab) approaches to investigate disease mechanisms, particularly in cancer immunology and genomics.

My research interests include:
- Cancer immunology
- Immunogenomics
- RNA-seq analysis
- Variant prioritization
- Tumor microenvironment
- Immunoinformatics

I have experience in both laboratory and computational research, including DNA extraction, PCR, molecular genetics techniques, RNA-seq analysis using DESeq2 and edgeR, and immunoinformatics-based vaccine design.

My long-term goal is to contribute to translational cancer research by combining experimental biology with computational analysis to better understand disease biology and therapeutic responses.
