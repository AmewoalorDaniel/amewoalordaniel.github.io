**I am a biomedical scientist interested in understanding how genomic and immunological alterations contribute to cancer development and how these insights can be translated into more effective diagnostics and therapies**. My research integrates experimental molecular biology with computational genomics and bioinformatics to study cancer susceptibility, tumour–immune interactions, and the broader genetic basis of disease.

A central motivation behind my work is the persistent underrepresentation of African populations in global genomics research. I am particularly interested in pursuing scientifically rigorous research that addresses questions directly relevant to African communities and contributes to a more inclusive understanding of human disease biology.

I am currently pursuing an MPhil in Biotechnology at Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology (KNUST), Ghana, where my research focuses on the shared genetic architecture between syndromic orofacial clefts and hereditary cancer susceptibility using whole exome sequencing.
